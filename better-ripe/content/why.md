At **Fri Mar 22 15:27:14 CET 2024** RIPE NCC mailed members with the subject **\[members‑discuss] RIPE NCC Charging Scheme 2025 Open House: Recording and Slides Available**. The thread ignited an unprecedented wave of replies on the *members‑discuss* list.

## What we heard

* Sharp fee increases unaccompanied by visible service improvements.
* Limited insight into the cost structure behind the 2025 draft charging scheme.
* Insufficient time for meaningful member input before adoption.

## Why this matters

RIPE NCC is a membership association, not a commercial vendor. Its charging model must be predictable, transparent, and equitable. Anything less erodes trust and, ultimately, the stability of Internet number‑resource governance in our region.

## Our proposal in brief

1. **Cost‑based, not volume‑based** fees that reflect the actual expense of the services each member consumes.
2. **Budget discipline:** cap annual operational‑expenditure growth at the average EU inflation rate unless members explicitly approve an exception.
3. **Full transparency:** publish a line‑item budget and quarterly spending reports.
4. **Member veto window:** introduce a 30‑day objection period before any fee change takes effect.
